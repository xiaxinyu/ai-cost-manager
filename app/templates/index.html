Forecast assumptions (7d)
Uses the last 28 valid daily points to fit a linear trend with day-of-week residual correction. Output is integer and clamped to ≥ 0.
  • Window: 28 samples (clamped to [2, 365])
  • Horizon: 7 days (clamped to [1, 30])
  • Continuity: dashed forecast starts at last actual point
